Useful Tips

Tips for Monitoring Pests

  • ✔️ Inspect crops regularly for signs of pests.
  • ✔️ Check the underside of leaves and plant stems.
  • ✔️ Use traps to monitor pest populations.

Tips for Early Detection

  • ✔️ Look for damaged leaves, holes, and unusual spots.
  • ✔️ Check plants frequently during the growing season.
  • ✔️ Identify pests correctly before taking action.

Tips for Effective Treatment of Pest Infestations

  • ✔️ Remove heavily infested plant parts when appropriate.
  • ✔️ Use biological and other targeted pest control methods.
  • ✔️ Apply approved pesticides only when necessary.
  • ✔️ Follow product label instructions and safety precautions.
